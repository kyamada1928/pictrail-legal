PicTavi (formerly PicTrail) is an iOS app that helps you view photos with location information on a map. Starting with version 1.1, the app is called PicTavi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are some photos not shown on the map?

PicTavi can show photos that contain location information. Photos without location metadata may not appear on the map.

Why do I see only some photos?

If you selected Limited Photo Access in iOS, PicTavi can only access the photos you selected. You can change photo access permissions in the iOS Settings app.

Does PicTavi upload my photos?

No. PicTavi processes photos and location metadata on your device. PicTavi does not upload your photos.

What is Full Scan?

Full Scan checks your photo library for photos with location information and stores only the metadata needed for map display on your device. Photo files themselves are not copied or uploaded.

Why is there an ad during Full Scan?

Starting with version 1.1, PicTavi shows one small banner ad on the Full Scan progress screen only. The scan does not wait for the ad and completes whether or not an ad is shown. Your photos and locations are never given to the advertising provider.

How do I change the language?

PicTavi follows your iPhone's language (Japanese or English; other languages use English). To use a different language only for PicTavi, open the Settings app, then Apps > PicTavi > Language. If Language is not shown, add the language under General > Language & Region first.
